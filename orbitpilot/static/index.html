MANUAL AXIS CONTROL

Hold to move · release to stop

Physical movement

Clear the full slew path, balance the mount and secure cables. These controls send real ASCOM MoveAxis commands. Release to stop; a 1.5-second server watchdog stops motion if the app loses contact. The app inhibits idle sleep while manual motion is active, but do not force Windows to sleep; stop the mount first.

Connect an ASCOM telescope or local Alpaca simulator to check its advertised axis capabilities and rates.
COORDINATE SLEW & TRACKING

Home is only available when the selected telescope driver advertises CanFindHome. Polar alignment requires physical adjustment of the mount's altitude/azimuth bolts; OrbitPilot can guide but cannot turn them.
